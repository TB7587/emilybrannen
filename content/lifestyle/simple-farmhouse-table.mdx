A pretty table doesn't need to be complicated. Mine comes together in about ten minutes, and most of it is already in the cabinet.

## My three rules

1. Start with something plain: a linen runner or bare wood.
2. Add one thing from outside. Greenery, branches, whatever is growing.
3. Keep it low so everyone can see each other.

That's it. The food and the people do the rest.
