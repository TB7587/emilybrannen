Sunday mornings are the one part of the week I guard. No alarms, no errands, nowhere to be.

## What it looks like

- Coffee on the porch before anyone else is up
- Something warm in the oven, usually banana bread
- Everybody at the table, phones in the basket by the door

> It isn't fancy. It's just the same few things, every week, and that's the whole point.

## Why it matters

The rest of the week moves fast. One slow morning is how we catch up with each other before it starts again.
