ingredients:
  - "4 cans corn"
  - "7 tablespoons butter"
  - "16 oz cream cheese"
  - "3/4 cup heavy whipping cream"
  - "Salt and pepper, to taste"
steps:
  - "Add the corn, butter, cream cheese and heavy whipping cream to a large pot."
  - "Cook over medium heat, stirring frequently, until the butter and cream cheese have melted and everything is fully combined."
  - "Season with salt and pepper to taste. Serve hot."
