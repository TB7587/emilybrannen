ingredients:
  - 2 tablespoons salted butter
  - 2 tablespoons honey
  - 1 bag (16 oz) frozen sweet corn
  - 2 oz cream cheese, cubed
  - 1/4 teaspoon salt
  - 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  - Chopped fresh parsley, for serving (optional)
steps:
  - Melt the butter and honey together in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  - Add the frozen corn and cook, stirring now and then, until hot and starting to turn golden, 5 to 8 minutes.
  - Add the cream cheese, salt and pepper. Stir until the cream cheese melts into a glossy sauce, about 2 minutes.
  - Taste, add a pinch more salt if it needs it, and sprinkle with parsley. Serve warm.

This is the side dish that disappears first at every single family dinner. It's sweet, a little creamy, and it takes **15 minutes** with a bag of frozen corn.

No need to thaw the corn first. Straight from the freezer to the skillet works best.
