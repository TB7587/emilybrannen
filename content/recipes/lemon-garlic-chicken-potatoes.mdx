description: Juicy lemon garlic chicken thighs and crispy potatoes roasted together on one sheet pan. An easy weeknight dinner with almost no cleanup.
ingredients:
  - 6 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
  - 1 1/2 lbs baby gold potatoes, halved
  - 3 tablespoons olive oil
  - 4 cloves garlic, minced
  - 1 lemon, juiced, plus 1 lemon, sliced
  - 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  - 1 teaspoon paprika
  - 1 teaspoon salt
  - 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 425°F.
  - In a large bowl, whisk the olive oil, garlic, lemon juice, oregano, paprika, salt and pepper.
  - Add the chicken and potatoes to the bowl and toss until everything is well coated.
  - Spread the potatoes on a rimmed sheet pan, cut side down. Nestle the chicken in, skin side up, and tuck the lemon slices around it.
  - Roast for 35 to 40 minutes, until the skin is crisp and the chicken reaches 165°F in the thickest part.
  - Rest for 5 minutes, then spoon the pan juices over the top and serve.

One pan, one bowl, and dinner is done. The potatoes roast in the chicken drippings, which is exactly as good as it sounds.

I make this on the busiest night of the week because the oven does all the work.
