ingredientsHeading: Cheesecake
ingredients:
  - "1 and 1/4 cups (300ml) heavy cream or heavy whipping cream"
  - "24 ounces (678g) full-fat brick cream cheese, softened to room temperature"
  - "1/2 cup (100g) granulated sugar"
  - "2 Tablespoons (15g) confectioners’ sugar"
  - "1/4 cup (60g) sour cream, at room temperature"
  - "2 teaspoons lemon juice"
  - "1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract"
steps:
  - "Using a stand mixer whip the heavy cream into stiff peaks on medium-high speed. Set aside."
  - "In your stand mixer, mix the cream cheese and granulated sugar together until smooth. Then add confectioners’ sugar, sour cream, lemon juice, and vanilla extract. Continue mixing 2–3 minutes on medium-high speed until smooth."
  - "Next you’re going to fold the whipped cream into the cheesecake filling until combined. This takes several turns of your silicone spatula. Combined carefully to not deflate the whipped cream."
  - "Once combined you’ll scoop the cheesecake filling into your pie crust, cover with plastic wrap and cool in the fridge for a few hours or overnight."

**for my Santa decorations, I made extra whipped cream, scooped it into a piping bag, and piped little mounds around the pie. Then I topped each topped one with a strawberry and a bit of whipped cream on top to complete his hat. Lastly, I placed a small nonpareil in the middle for his nose!
