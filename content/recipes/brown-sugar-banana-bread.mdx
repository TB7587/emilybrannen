description: Soft, moist brown sugar banana bread made in one bowl with no mixer. The best way to use up spotty bananas.
ingredients:
  - 3 very ripe bananas
  - 1/2 cup (1 stick) butter, melted
  - 3/4 cup packed brown sugar
  - 2 large eggs
  - 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  - 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  - 1 teaspoon baking soda
  - 1/2 teaspoon salt
  - 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 350°F. Grease a 9x5-inch loaf pan.
  - Mash the bananas in a large bowl until mostly smooth.
  - Stir in the melted butter and brown sugar, then the eggs and vanilla.
  - Add the flour, baking soda, salt and cinnamon. Stir just until no dry flour remains. A few lumps are fine.
  - Pour into the loaf pan and bake for 55 to 60 minutes, until a toothpick in the center comes out clean.
  - Cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then move to a rack to cool before slicing.

The spottier the bananas, the better the bread. This one is a single bowl, no mixer, and it makes the whole house smell like a bakery.

It's even better the next day, if it lasts that long.
