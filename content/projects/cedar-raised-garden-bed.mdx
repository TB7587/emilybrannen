description: Build a simple 4x8 cedar raised garden bed in one afternoon with basic tools. A beginner-friendly weekend project.
materials:
  - 6 cedar boards, 2x6 x 8 ft
  - 1 cedar post, 4x4 x 4 ft (cut into four 11-inch corner posts)
  - 1 box of 3-inch exterior deck screws
  - Cardboard or landscape fabric for the bottom
  - About 1 cubic yard of raised bed soil
steps:
  - Cut two of the 8 ft boards in half to make four 4 ft end boards. Leave the other four boards at 8 ft for the long sides.
  - Cut the 4x4 post into four 11-inch corner posts.
  - Screw two 4 ft boards to a pair of corner posts, one stacked on the other, to make an end panel. Repeat for the second end.
  - Stand the end panels up and screw the long boards to the corner posts, two boards high on each side.
  - Set the bed in a sunny, level spot and check that the corners are square.
  - Line the bottom with cardboard, fill with soil, water well and plant.

If you can drive a screw, you can build this. It's the first garden bed we ever made, and it's still the one I'd tell a beginner to start with.

Cedar costs a little more than pine, but it holds up for years without any sealer.
