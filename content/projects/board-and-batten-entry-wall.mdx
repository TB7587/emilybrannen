materials:
  - Primed 1x4 boards for the top rail and battens
  - Primed 1x6 board for the baseboard (or keep your existing one)
  - Construction adhesive
  - 2-inch brad nails
  - Paintable caulk and wood filler
  - 1 quart of satin or semi-gloss paint
  - Wall hooks
steps:
  - Measure the wall and decide on the height of the top rail. About two-thirds of the way up the wall looks best.
  - Mark the wall studs, then nail the top rail in place, checking it with a level.
  - Divide the wall into even sections and mark where each batten will go. Aim for 14 to 18 inches apart.
  - Cut the battens to fit between the baseboard and the top rail. Glue and nail each one, checking for plumb.
  - Fill the nail holes, caulk every seam where wood meets wall, and let it dry.
  - Paint the boards and the wall between them the same color, two coats.
  - Add hooks along the top rail once the paint has cured.
